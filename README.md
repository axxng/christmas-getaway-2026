# Christmas Getaway Fares

Round-trip fares from Singapore for a short Christmas trip (out Wed 23 Dec, back Sat 26 Dec 2026), priced on Google Flights.

Live page: https://axxng.github.io/christmas-getaway-2026/

- Two tables: under SGD 1,000, and SGD 1,000 to 1,500, with flight numbers and local times for every leg
- A Japan section: 12 cities in detail and 11 more checked, each priced for a Saturday 26 or Sunday 27 return, with tabs on each card to compare the two
- A side-by-side table of prices on the airline itself, Trip.com, Expedia, Booking.com, Agoda and Traveloka
- A struck-off table for destinations that went over budget or had no sensible routing
- A rough itinerary for every destination in both tables, timed around the flights
- Version tabs: every update is kept, so you can flip back to any earlier day's prices

How each update is done: [RITUAL.md](RITUAL.md).

Osaka and Chengdu fares were last checked on 28 Nov 2026, the other Japan cities on 23 Nov 2026, and the other tables on 22 Nov 2026. All will have moved since.
