About the sellers

  • Trusted here means the airline itself, Trip.com, Expedia, Booking.com, Agoda or Traveloka.
  • Gotogate, Mytrip and Flightnetwork are all run by Etraveli Group, which also handles flight bookings for Booking.com.
  • The others (hutchgo.com, Jettzy, Cheaptickets.sg, Teaflight, Kiwi.com, eDreams, ly.com, Alitrip, Airpaz, Adam Vacations, Travomint, wego) I haven't vetted. Some save SGD 10–30; Perth's saves about SGD 100.
  • Booking through any agent means changes and refunds go through the agent, not the airline.

Before you book

  • "Two tickets" means the outbound and return are booked separately. If one flight is cancelled, the other airline has no duty to rebook you.
  • Agent fares (Trip.com, Kiwi.com and others) are usually cheapest but harder to change. Airline prices are the safer reference.
  • Times are local. +1 means the next day.
  • Christmas events listed are what each city usually runs. Check 2026 dates.
